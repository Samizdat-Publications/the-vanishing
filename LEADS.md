## B. Analogue cases -- scientists whose careers or lives ended after they became inconvenient

**These are the highest-value discovery category and the current process had no query that
reached them.** Each needs verification before it goes anywhere near the tracker.

| Lead | Status | Why it is a lead |
|---|---|---|
| **James E. McDonald** | **unverified, highest interest** | Senior physicist, Institute of Atmospheric Physics, **University of Arizona**. The most scientifically credentialed public advocate of his era; his professional credibility was attacked and collapsed; he died in 1971. **Verify the circumstances before asserting anything.** Two reasons he matters: he is a near-exact structural analogue of the neutralisation frame, and the University of Arizona tie connects to Michael Hicks, whose only located photograph is on a U of A LPL memoriam page. |
| **Capt. Uyrangê Hollanda Lima** | unverified | Brazilian Air Force, commander of Operação Prato. Died 1997, reportedly after giving testimony about the operation. Non-US, so out of the tracker's stated scope, but relevant as a pattern instance. **Verify circumstances.** |
| **Betty Cash** | unverified | Lead plaintiff, *Cash v. United States*, claimed radiation injuries from a military-associated aerial encounter. Relevant to the injury-and-incapacitation branch of the thesis rather than to death. |
