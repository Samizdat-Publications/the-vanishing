## NEW Cases Added (6 new entries)

### 1. Ning Li (DEAD - Jul 27, 2021)
- **Age:** 78
- **Location:** Huntsville, Alabama
- **Affiliation:** University of Alabama Huntsville / AC Gravity LLC
- **Details:** Chinese-American physicist known for anti-gravity research. In the 1990s at UAH, co-authored papers on producing anti-gravity effects through rotating ions creating a gravitomagnetic field. In 1999 left UAH to form AC Gravity LLC with DoD funding. Went completely dark; no publications after 2003. Died July 27, 2021.
- **Why it matters:** Earliest case in the pattern. Huntsville cluster. Anti-gravity/exotic propulsion research directly relevant to UAP reverse-engineering hypothesis.
- **Sources:** Wikipedia (Ning Li physicist), Huntsville Business Journal, Berryhill Funeral Home obituary

### 2. Amy Eskridge (DEAD - Jun 11, 2022)
- **Age:** 34
- **Location:** Huntsville, Alabama
- **Affiliation:** Institute for Exotic Science (co-founder), plasma physicist
- **Cause:** Officially self-inflicted gunshot wound
- **Details:** Co-founded the Institute for Exotic Science focusing on experimental propulsion including "antigravity" research. One month before death, texted a friend: "I definitely did not plan to kill myself." Claimed to have been struck by a "directed energy weapon." A former British intelligence officer told NewsNation she feared for her life due to her propulsion research.
- **Why it matters:** Second Huntsville case. Her father (former NASA employee) insists nothing suspicious. But the text message and directed energy claims make this deeply anomalous.
- **Sources:** NewsNation, Newsweek, Cybernews, Fox News

### 3. Michael David Hicks (DEAD - Jul 30, 2023)
- **Age:** 59
- **Location:** Sunland, California
- **Affiliation:** NASA JPL (1998-2022)
- **Details:** Worked on NASA's Deep Space 1 mission and the DART asteroid-deflection project. Over 80 scientific papers. Specialized in comets and asteroids. No cause of death was made public. Third JPL-connected death/disappearance in the pattern.
- **Why it matters:** Earliest JPL case. No cause of death disclosed. Now the third JPL scientist on the list alongside Maiwald and Reza.
- **Sources:** Newsweek, CNN, NewsNation, Union Leader

### 4. Matthew James Sullivan (DEAD - May 12, 2024)
- **Age:** 39
- **Location:** Falls Church, Virginia
- **Affiliation:** Former USAF Intelligence Officer; NSA; National Air & Space Intelligence Center (NASIC)
- **Cause:** Accidental drug overdose from multiple medications
- **Details:** Deployed to CENTCOM and Indo-Pacific Command. Bronze Star recipient (Operation Enduring Freedom). ### 5. Joshua LeBlanc (DEAD - Jul 22, 2025)
