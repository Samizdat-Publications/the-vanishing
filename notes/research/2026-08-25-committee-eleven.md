## The headline finding, and it cuts against us

**The committee did not compile an independent list. It cited press reporting.**

All four Oversight letters (FBI, NASA, DOE, Department of War, all published 20 April 2026)
footnote their enumeration to:

- Chris Melore, "Missing nuclear official becomes TENTH person tied to dark pattern
  surrounding US secrets," **Daily Mail, 11 April 2026** (footnotes 1 and 6)
- Ross O'Keefe, **NY Post, 9 April 2026** (footnote 2)
- **NY Post, 18 and 21 March 2026** (footnotes 4 and 5)

**This is not an external curation check. It is a check on the same media ecosystem the
tracker reads.** An eleven-for-eleven match between two readers of the same four articles
is not independent corroboration. **Presenting it as independent corroboration would be the
single most damaging thing the tracker could do with this finding.** Say so wherever the
diff is used.
